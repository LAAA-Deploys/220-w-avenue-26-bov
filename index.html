Executive Summary

220 W Avenue 26 is a 10,633 SF lot on Avenue 26 in Lincoln Heights, improved with a one-story 3,381 SF building from 1937 that has served as a veterinary hospital. It is zoned UV(CA), the Urban Village zone of the Cornfield Arroyo Seco Specific Plan (CASP), which allows residential, commercial and light industrial uses in the same building, with no limit on unit count.

The site is about 0.2 miles from the Lincoln/Cypress station on the Metro L Line, in a Transit Priority Area and a federal Opportunity Zone. Our reading of the plan's maps gives it a maximum FAR of 3:1, about 31,900 SF of building, and heights up to a 60 ft average. A by-right mixed-use project of up to 15 units fits on the lot, and the plan's affordable housing bonus opens a larger building. Four 100% affordable projects have been approved or filed within a mile since 2024, so affordable developers, market-rate builders and owner-users of the existing building all make up the buyer pool.

What the Zoning Allows

The site is governed by the 2013 Cornfield Arroyo Seco Specific Plan, which ZIMAS still shows for this parcel. The Urban Village zone permits residential, commercial and light industrial uses together and sets building size by floor area rather than unit count.
